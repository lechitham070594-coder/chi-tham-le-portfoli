Marketing Data Workflow

Self-created demonstration · Spreadsheet operations

1. Define the brief

Confirm target industry, geography, required fields, qualification rules, source restrictions and delivery format.

2. Collect and cite

Use public or client-authorized sources. Save the source URL and date checked for each record. Do not guess missing contact details.

3. Standardize

Use consistent company names, city names, phone formats, categories and status labels. Keep original source wording when needed for auditability.

4. Quality check

Check duplicate names/domains, missing required fields, broken links and records outside the agreed target. Mark uncertain entries for review.

5. Segment and prioritize

Apply the agreed criteria and explain why each record fits. Avoid unsupported scoring or assumptions about buying intent.

6. Deliver and hand over

Provide the spreadsheet, a short summary, source notes and a list of limitations or follow-up questions.
