Working principles

  • Use public or client-authorized information; respect privacy and platform terms.
  • Record source links and research dates where applicable.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions and items needing confirmation.
  • Follow the agreed scope, file format and deadline.
  • Do not fabricate contact details, client experience or performance results.
